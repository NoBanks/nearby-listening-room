Nearby Listening Room

Sit down. Bring your hands. The music of NoBanks Nearby.

On this screen: click a record to play it. Space pauses, H lets the host pick.

The host runs on Google Gemini. What you ask it is sent to Gemini to answer you, and Google may use it to improve its products, so don't share anything private. A few short notes stay in this browser so the host remembers you next time.


Optional. Hold the radio and speak. Only that clip is sent to Google Gemini to understand you. This room doesn't keep it.
